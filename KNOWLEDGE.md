## 2. Story, mission, vision, values

**Origin.** Businesses rarely have just one technology problem: a network that fails, software that does not fit, data that is not safe, a website that does not sell, a team losing hours on repetitive tasks. Each problem usually belongs to a different supplier and nobody owns the whole picture. Supplies and Works decided to be the team that owns the whole picture. It started with infrastructure and IT services, then grew into software, cloud, design and automation, and built its own products (Badibo, Kira) now used by paying customers.

**Mission.** To be the solution to the technology problems of businesses and individuals, with one team they can trust from the first idea to the daily running of their systems.

**Vision.**
- In 5 years: a fully remote company serving clients across the world, with North America as a priority market.
- In 10 years: offices in other countries, closer to the people and businesses we serve.

**Values.**
1. **Pragmatism over hype.** We recommend what works for your business, not what is fashionable.
2. **Ownership.** We own the outcome, not only the tasks.
3. **Craft.** Clean code, careful design, tidy cabling.
4. **Honesty.** We say what we can do, what we cannot, and what we recommend. We never overpromise.
5. **Long-term thinking.** We build for the third year, not only for launch day.

**Working principles.** We review each other's work · we document what we build · we tell you early when something is at risk · we keep learning.

## 4. The five studios (services)

### 01 · Cloud Studio
Cloud architecture, migration, cloud-native development, hybrid cloud, backup and disaster recovery. *Keywords:* AWS · Migration · Backup · Resilience.
- **Cloud engineering:** architecture and design, staged migrations with rollback plans, cloud-native development (autoscaling, managed DBs, queues, serverless, CI/CD), hybrid cloud, infrastructure as code (**Terraform, AWS CloudFormation/CDK, GitHub Actions**), monitoring and monthly cost/performance reviews.
- **Focus:** AWS first (founder is an **AWS Certified Solutions Architect**); also Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud.
- **Security practice:** least privilege, encryption in transit and at rest, separated environments, audit logs, regular access reviews; **MFA on every account and AWS CloudTrail audit logging from day one**. No ISO 27001 / SOC 2 certification (never claim it).
- **Engagement:** Assess (1–2 weeks) → Plan → Implement → Operate.
- **Deliverables:** architecture diagrams, environment setup, documentation, security configuration, monitoring dashboards, runbooks, handover session, optional support plan.

### Flagship offer — Backup & Disaster Recovery (Cloud Studio · Protect)
Automatic, encrypted, tested backups from on-site servers to the cloud, with a written recovery plan.
- **Why:** hardware fails, accidents happen, disasters strike, ransomware is common, and copies are rare.
- **What we set up:** automatic backup, encryption and access control, versioning and retention, restore testing, disaster recovery plan, daily monitoring and alerts, a monthly report.
- **RPO** = how much data you can afford to lose; **RTO** = how long you can afford to be down. Set together with the client first.
- **Recommended:** local copy + cloud copy.
- **Process:** Review → Define → Connect → Automate → Test → Monitor.
- **For:** clinics, pharmacies, schools, accounting/law offices, retailers, distributors, restaurants, hotels, NGOs, factories, any business with its own servers.
- **Plans:** Essential / Business / Critical `[features, prices]`.

## 11. Voice and messaging rules

- **Tone:** confident, warm, precise. Premium but human. Proud of being African, **never apologetic**.
- Plain language, short sentences, active voice. Explain outcomes, not jargon.
- Say "we" (the team) and "you" (the client).
- Africa is part of the story: talent, resilience, time-zone advantage, pride. Never folkloric, never "despite being African".
- Bilingual: write English first; French versions are welcome for Cameroon/France/Canada audiences.
- **Never claim:** certifications we don't hold (only AWS Solutions Architect is real), fake numbers, invented clients or testimonials, 24/7 support, guaranteed results.
- Busting myths (useful for content):
  - "Remote teams in Africa are risky" → risk comes from unclear scope, not geography.
  - "Power and connectivity are unreliable" → we plan for it (backup connections and power, cloud tools).
  - "You won't know our market" → we study your business first.
