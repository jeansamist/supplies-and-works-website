### The risk is real
**Hardware fails.** Disks and servers wear out.
**Accidents happen.** A deleted folder, a spilled drink, a power surge.
**Disasters strike.** Fire, flood, theft.
**Attacks are common.** Ransomware locks files and demands payment.
**Copies are rare.** Many businesses rely on one drive, one server, one person’s memory.
If your only copy of critical data lives in one place, you are one incident away from days or weeks of downtime.

### What we set up
**1. Automatic backup**
Your files, databases and systems are copied to secure cloud storage on a schedule you choose. No manual steps.

**2. Encryption and access control**
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Only authorized people can access it.

**3. Versioning and retention**
Keep several versions, so you can go back to yesterday, last week or last month, including before a ransomware attack.

**4. Restore testing**
We run restore drills and report the results. A backup is only valuable if it restores.

**5. Disaster recovery plan**
A written, practical plan: who does what, in what order, with which tools, to get back to work. [Optional: a standby environment in the cloud to restart critical systems.]

**6. Monitoring and alerts**
We monitor backup jobs every day and fix failures before they matter.

**7. Reporting**
A monthly report on backup status, storage use and restore tests.

**RPO (Recovery Point Objective)**: how much data you can afford to lose, for example one hour or one day.
**RTO (Recovery Time Objective)**: how long you can afford to be down, for example one hour or one day.
We set these with you first, then design the solution to meet them. A clinic and a small shop do not need the same targets.
